Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

SignalCheck is a browser extension that analyzes webpage content to detect AI-generated text and verify domain reputation. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What We Collect

When you click "Scan Page", SignalCheck collects:

  • Page URL — to check domain reputation via Google Safe Browsing
  • Page text content — visible text from the current webpage (up to 2,000 characters) for AI content analysis
We do NOT collect: browsing history, personal information, passwords, financial data, or any data from pages you don't explicitly scan.

How We Use It

Collected data is used solely to:

  • Analyze text for AI-generated content patterns using Google Gemini
  • Check domain reputation against Google Safe Browsing
  • Return a trust score to you

Data Storage

  • Scan data is stored temporarily on our server for up to 24 hours
  • After 24 hours, scan records are automatically deleted
  • We do not create user accounts or persistent profiles

Third-Party Services

SignalCheck uses:

  • Google Gemini API — for AI content analysis (text is sent to Google's servers)
  • Google Safe Browsing API — for domain reputation checks

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share your data with any other third parties.

Your Control

  • The extension only activates when you click "Scan Page"
  • You choose which pages to scan
  • Uninstalling the extension stops all data collection
